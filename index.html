One door
of two

167 Mott Street, west side, between Broome and Grand. Two small shopfronts under a seven-storey walk-up. One is a candle studio open till ten every night. The other is on the market. Every building here is real, at its recorded height. The brick one with the green shopfronts is the door.

Tenants and owners

Who has been here

One family held the building for 45 years. Then it sold twice in 15 months, and the shopfronts were rebuilt in between.
